Carnatic 78 RPM Gramophone Records

A catalogue of early recordings of South Indian music on 78 RPM gramophone records

About

This catalogue documents early Carnatic (South Indian classical) music recordings on 78 RPM gramophone records, spanning 1905–1916. The data is sourced from the Gramophone Company Discography. AI has been used to generate several entries including song identifications, ragam/talam, and composer attributions. Although every effort has been made to ensure accuracy, there may be some discrepancies in these results.
